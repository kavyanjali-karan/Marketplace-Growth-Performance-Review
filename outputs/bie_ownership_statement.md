# Business Intelligence Engineering Ownership Statement

## Marketplace Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention Program

### Purpose

This document defines the responsibilities, ownership boundaries, governance expectations, and accountability model for Business Intelligence Engineering within the Marketplace organization.

The objective is to clearly distinguish between metric ownership, business ownership, operational ownership, and executive accountability.

Business Intelligence Engineering exists to enable better decisions through trusted, governed, and scalable analytics.

---

# Business Intelligence Engineering Mission

Provide accurate, trusted, scalable, and actionable business intelligence that enables leadership teams to make faster and better business decisions.

Business Intelligence Engineering is responsible for ensuring decision-makers understand:

What is happening.

Why it is happening.

What business impact exists.

Where intervention is required.

# Marketplace Operating Model

Business Intelligence Engineering functions as the organization's measurement system.

Business Teams function as the organization's execution system.

Leadership functions as the organization's decision system.

The effectiveness of the organization depends on all three systems operating together.

---

# Success Criteria

Business Intelligence Engineering is successful when:

Leadership trusts reported metrics.

Performance risks are identified early.

Metrics remain consistent.

Business reviews drive action.

Decision quality improves.

Business teams receive actionable insights.

Strategic risks become visible before significant impact occurs.

---

# Executive Statement

Business Intelligence Engineering is not responsible for creating dashboards.

Business Intelligence Engineering is responsible for enabling better business decisions.

The purpose of reporting is not visibility.

The purpose of reporting is action.

The purpose of analytics is not analysis.

The purpose of analytics is business impact.

This ownership framework ensures that marketplace performance measurement remains trusted, scalable, governed, and aligned with executive decision-making.
