# Marketplace Business Review Operating Mechanism

## Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention Program

### Purpose

This operating mechanism defines how marketplace performance is monitored, reviewed, escalated, and acted upon across leadership levels.

The objective is to convert business intelligence outputs into repeatable business decisions.

This framework establishes accountability, reporting cadence, escalation procedures, and executive review standards.

---

# Business Intelligence Mission

Provide trusted, governed, and actionable performance intelligence that enables leadership teams to improve revenue growth, customer retention, operational excellence, and marketplace scalability.

Business Intelligence Engineering is responsible for ensuring decision-makers have accurate and timely performance visibility.

---

Late Delivery Rate

---

## Required Actions

Review SLA exceptions.

Investigate delivery failures.

Escalate unresolved operational issues.

---

## Escalation Thresholds

Delivery SLA below target.

Late deliveries increasing for three consecutive reporting periods.

# Success Criteria

The operating mechanism is successful when:

Leadership uses KPI reporting for decision-making.

Business risks are identified before significant impact occurs.

Metric definitions remain consistent across teams.

Operational issues are escalated rapidly.

Business reviews drive measurable business actions.
