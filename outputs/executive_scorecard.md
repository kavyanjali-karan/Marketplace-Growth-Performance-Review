# Executive Summary

Marketplace growth remains healthy; however, growth quality indicators suggest increasing dependence on customer acquisition and a limited number of high-performing categories.

Retention remains the largest revenue expansion opportunity.

Operational reliability continues to influence future customer value and long-term marketplace performance.

Leadership focus should prioritize retention-driven growth, customer experience improvement, and category diversification.

# Executive Conclusion

Marketplace growth remains strong; however, future performance will increasingly depend on the organization's ability to retain customers, maintain operational excellence, and diversify revenue generation.

The largest opportunity is not acquiring additional customers.

The largest opportunity is generating greater long-term value from customers already acquired.

Leadership priorities should focus on retention-driven growth, operational reliability, and strategic category expansion.
