# Executive Summary

This analysis was conducted to simulate an Amazon Business Analyst workflow using marketplace transaction data.

Key findings indicate:

1. Revenue is concentrated among a small percentage of customers.
2. High-performing product categories drive a disproportionate share of sales.
3. Customer retention opportunities exist within at-risk segments.
4. Funnel bottlenecks reduce conversion efficiency.
5. Lifecycle-based customer strategies can improve long-term marketplace performance.

Recommended actions include:

- Retaining high-value customers
- Re-engaging at-risk customers
- Investing in top-performing categories
- Improving conversion funnel efficiency
- Increasing customer review participation
