# Marketplace KPI Dictionary

## Purpose

This document establishes the official source of truth for all marketplace performance metrics used in reporting, dashboards, business reviews, and executive decision-making.

Every KPI included in executive reporting must have a documented business definition, owner, reporting cadence, and interpretation framework.

---

# Executive KPI Layer

## Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)

### Business Definition

Total value generated from completed marketplace purchases.

### Business Purpose

Measures overall marketplace scale and revenue generation.

### Executive Consumer

VP Marketplace

### Business Owner

Marketplace Leadership

### Technical Owner

Business Intelligence Engineering

### Reporting Cadence

Weekly
Monthly
Quarterly

### Interpretation

Increasing GMV indicates marketplace expansion and revenue growth.

---

## Revenue Growth Rate

### Business Definition

Percentage change in marketplace revenue compared with the previous reporting period.

### Business Purpose

Measures growth velocity.

### Executive Consumer

VP Marketplace

### Business Owner

Marketplace Leadership

### Reporting Cadence

Monthly
Quarterly

### Interpretation

Positive growth indicates marketplace expansion.

Declining growth indicates potential demand, retention, or operational challenges.

---

## Active Customers

# Governance Standard

No KPI may be presented in executive reporting without:

* Business definition
* Assigned owner
* Reporting cadence
* Validation process
* Source-of-truth dataset

This document serves as the official KPI reference for all marketplace reporting.
