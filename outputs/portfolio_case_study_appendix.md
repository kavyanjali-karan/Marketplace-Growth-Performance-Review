# Portfolio Case Study Appendix

## Marketplace Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention Program

### Business Intelligence Engineering Case Study

---

# Project Overview

## Business Context

Marketplace leadership observed continued revenue growth but lacked visibility into the sustainability of that growth.

While transaction volume remained healthy, leadership did not have a standardized framework to understand:

* Customer retention performance
* Revenue concentration risk
* Customer lifetime value
* Operational impact on future revenue
* Marketplace growth sustainability

Business Intelligence Engineering was tasked with creating a performance intelligence framework capable of supporting executive decision-making.

---

# Business Problem Statement

Revenue growth alone does not provide sufficient insight into marketplace health.

Leadership required a system capable of identifying:

Which customers create the most value.

Which customers are at risk of churn.

Which operational issues threaten future revenue.

Which categories drive growth.

Where future revenue opportunities exist.

The challenge was not measuring performance.

The challenge was enabling better decisions.

---

# Constraints

Data available through marketplace transaction records.

No direct access to marketing attribution data.

No direct access to inventory planning systems.

Historical analysis limited to available marketplace records.

Operational performance measured through delivery-related fields.

Despite these constraints, sufficient information existed to evaluate marketplace performance and identify growth opportunities.

---

# Key Analytical Decisions

## Decision 1

Prioritize Customer Retention Over Acquisition Analysis

Reason

Retention creates larger long-term revenue impact than acquisition volume alone.

Expected Benefit

Higher business relevance.

---

## Decision 2

Evaluate Revenue Quality Rather Than Revenue Volume

Reason

Revenue growth without retention can mask future business risks.

Expected Benefit

Improved executive visibility.

---

## Decision 3

Incorporate Operational Performance Into Revenue Analysis

Reason

Customer experience directly influences future purchasing behavior.

Expected Benefit

Cross-functional decision support.

---

## Decision 4

Analyze Revenue Concentration Risk

Reason

Marketplace dependence on limited categories creates strategic risk.

Expected Benefit

Improved growth sustainability.

---

# Tradeoffs

## Tradeoff

Broader Coverage vs Executive Focus

Decision

Prioritized executive decision support.

Reason

Leadership requires clarity rather than excessive detail.

---

## Tradeoff

Metric Quantity vs Metric Quality

Decision

Focused on a smaller number of high-value KPIs.

Reason

Executive reviews require prioritization.

---

## Tradeoff

Exploration vs Governance

Decision

Emphasized KPI standardization.

Reason

Consistent metrics improve decision quality.

Business Impact

Retention initiatives should prioritize these customers.

# Lessons Learned

Revenue growth alone is an incomplete measure of marketplace health.

Customer retention often provides greater long-term leverage than acquisition.

Operational performance directly influences future revenue.

Metric governance is essential for trusted reporting.

Executive reporting should prioritize decisions rather than metrics.

Business Intelligence Engineering creates value when analytics directly influence business actions.

# Executive Summary

This project transformed raw marketplace transaction data into a decision-support system designed for executive leadership.

The focus was not analysis.

The focus was enabling leadership to understand marketplace performance, identify growth opportunities, quantify risks, and make better business decisions.

The resulting framework demonstrates how Business Intelligence Engineering supports organizational decision-making through trusted metrics, governance, diagnostics, and executive reporting.
