# Quarterly Business Review Presentation Script

## Marketplace Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention Program

Presenter

Business Intelligence Engineering

Audience

VP Marketplace

Director Customer Growth

Director Operations

Director Category Management

Review Period

Q2 FY2026

---

# Opening Statement

Good morning everyone.

Today's review focuses on marketplace performance, customer retention, operational effectiveness, and future revenue risk.

The objective of this review is not simply to assess what happened during the quarter.

The objective is to identify the most significant drivers of future marketplace growth and determine where leadership intervention will generate the greatest business impact.

Overall marketplace performance remains healthy.

Revenue growth remains positive.

Customer acquisition remains strong.

However, growth quality indicators reveal several risks that require attention.

The most important finding from this review is that retention remains the largest opportunity for future revenue expansion.

---

# Marketplace Performance Overview

Marketplace demand remains healthy.

Customer activity remains strong.

Revenue generation continues to increase.

These indicators suggest the marketplace continues to attract customers and generate transactions successfully.

However, growth quality measures reveal a different story.

A substantial portion of future growth remains dependent on customer acquisition rather than customer retention.

This creates long-term efficiency risks and limits customer lifetime value expansion.

The question facing leadership is no longer how to acquire customers.

The question is how to maximize value from customers already acquired.

---

# Customer Retention Review

Customer retention remains the most significant opportunity identified during this review.

Analysis indicates returning customers contribute disproportionately to marketplace value.

Customers who make repeat purchases generate significantly more long-term revenue than customers who purchase only once.

This creates a clear strategic opportunity.

Improving repeat purchase behavior represents one of the most effective methods available to increase marketplace revenue without proportionally increasing acquisition investment.

Leadership should view retention not as a marketing initiative.

Leadership should view retention as a revenue growth initiative.

---

# Customer Lifetime Value Review

Customer lifetime value remains below its long-term potential.

The primary driver is not customer acquisition performance.

The primary driver is purchasing frequency.

Customers are entering the marketplace successfully.

The challenge is increasing the frequency with which they return.

Improving repeat purchase behavior will increase customer lifetime value, strengthen revenue retention, and improve overall marketplace economics.

This remains a strategic priority.

---

# Revenue Risk Review

Three major risks were identified during this review.

Risk One.

Customer acquisition dependency.

A large portion of future revenue growth remains dependent on continuously acquiring new customers.

This increases growth costs and creates long-term efficiency concerns.

Risk Two.

Revenue concentration.

A limited number of categories generate a disproportionate share of marketplace revenue.

This concentration creates vulnerability should category performance deteriorate.

Risk Three.

Operational reliability.

Delivery performance continues to influence future customer behavior and repeat purchasing activity.

While current performance remains acceptable, continued monitoring is required.

---

# Category Performance Review

Category performance remains uneven.

Several categories continue to drive the majority of marketplace revenue.

This creates growth concentration risk.

A diversified marketplace is generally more resilient than a concentrated marketplace.

Leadership should continue supporting high-performing categories while accelerating investment in emerging categories capable of generating future growth.

The objective is not reducing performance among leading categories.

The objective is reducing dependency on them.

---

# Customer Experience Review

Customer experience remains closely linked to retention outcomes.

Operational performance directly affects customer satisfaction and future purchasing behavior.

Delivery reliability continues to serve as a leading indicator of customer retention.

Customers experiencing delivery failures demonstrate lower future engagement and reduced repeat purchase activity.

Operational excellence should therefore be viewed as a growth driver rather than purely an operational objective.

Improving delivery performance protects future revenue.

---

# Strategic Opportunities

Four growth opportunities were identified.

Opportunity One.

Customer Retention Expansion.

This represents the highest-impact opportunity available.

Improving repeat purchase behavior will increase revenue retention and customer lifetime value.

Opportunity Two.

High Value Customer Protection.

A relatively small segment contributes a significant share of marketplace revenue.

Protecting these relationships reduces future revenue volatility.

Opportunity Three.

Category Diversification.

Reducing category concentration improves long-term marketplace resilience.

Opportunity Four.

Delivery Experience Improvement.

Improving operational reliability strengthens customer satisfaction and retention.

Together these opportunities represent the most significant drivers of future marketplace performance.

---

# Recommended Leadership Actions

Recommendation One.

Approve customer retention as a primary growth initiative.

Expected Outcome.

Improved repeat purchase rate and customer lifetime value.

---

Recommendation Two.

Establish operational accountability for delivery SLA compliance.

Expected Outcome.

Improved customer experience and reduced churn risk.

---

Recommendation Three.

Increase investment in emerging categories.

Expected Outcome.

Reduced concentration risk and stronger marketplace diversification.

---

Recommendation Four.

Implement quarterly revenue risk reviews.

Expected Outcome.

Earlier identification of growth threats and more proactive decision making.

---

# Leadership Decisions Required

Today we request leadership approval for the following actions.

Approval of customer retention acceleration initiatives.

Approval of category diversification planning.

Approval of delivery performance ownership framework.

Approval of quarterly revenue risk monitoring.

These actions address the most significant opportunities identified during this review.

---

# Closing Statement

To conclude.

Marketplace performance remains healthy.

Revenue continues to grow.

Customer acquisition remains effective.

However, future growth will increasingly depend on our ability to retain customers, improve operational reliability, and diversify revenue generation.

The most important conclusion from this review is straightforward.

The largest opportunity is not acquiring more customers.

The largest opportunity is generating more value from customers already acquired.

If leadership successfully improves retention, customer experience, and category diversification, marketplace growth will become more sustainable, more efficient, and more resilient over time.

Thank you.

I welcome questions and discussion.
