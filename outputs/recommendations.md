# Strategic Initiatives and Business Impact Plan

## Marketplace Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention Program

### Purpose

This document translates analytical findings into business initiatives, ownership structures, success metrics, and expected marketplace impact.

The objective is to ensure insights result in measurable business outcomes.

---

# Initiative 1

## Customer Retention Acceleration Program

### Business Problem

A significant share of customers complete only one purchase, limiting customer lifetime value and increasing dependence on customer acquisition.

### Strategic Objective

Increase repeat purchase behavior and improve revenue generated from existing customers.

### Executive Sponsor

VP Marketplace

### Business Owner

Director Customer Growth

### Supporting Teams

* CRM
* Lifecycle Marketing
* Customer Analytics
* BI Engineering

### Success Metrics

Primary

* Repeat Purchase Rate
* Revenue Retention Rate

Secondary

* Customer Lifetime Value
* Average Orders Per Customer

### Expected Business Outcome

Improved customer retention increases long-term revenue generation without proportional increases in acquisition spending.

### Review Cadence

Monthly Business Review

### Priority

HIGH

# Executive Conclusion

The largest growth opportunity identified is not customer acquisition.

The largest growth opportunity is increasing value generated from customers already acquired.

Retention improvement, operational excellence, and category diversification represent the highest-impact levers available to marketplace leadership.
