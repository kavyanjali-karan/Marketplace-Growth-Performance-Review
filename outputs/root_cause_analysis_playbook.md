# KPI Root Cause Analysis Playbook

## Marketplace Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention Program

### Purpose

This playbook establishes the standard investigation framework used by Business Intelligence Engineering whenever a KPI deviates from expected performance.

The objective is to ensure performance deterioration is diagnosed consistently, escalated appropriately, and resolved through data-driven decision making.

This framework is used during:

* Daily Operational Reviews
* Weekly Business Reviews
* Monthly Business Reviews
* Quarterly Business Reviews

---

# Root Cause Analysis Principles

Every KPI deterioration must answer five questions:

What changed?

Where did it change?

When did it change?

Why did it change?

What action is required?

No KPI investigation is considered complete until a business owner has been assigned and corrective actions have been documented.

# Business Intelligence Engineering Responsibility

Business Intelligence Engineering owns:

Performance Monitoring

Variance Detection

Diagnostic Analysis

Executive Reporting

Risk Identification

Decision Support

Business Intelligence Engineering does not own business outcomes.

Business Intelligence Engineering owns identifying performance issues early enough that business teams can act before significant impact occurs.

---

# Success Criteria

The playbook is successful when:

Performance deterioration is identified quickly.

Root causes are validated using data.

Business owners are assigned.

Corrective actions are implemented.

Leadership decisions are supported by evidence.

Operational and strategic risks are reduced.

---

# Executive Conclusion

The purpose of KPI reporting is not to identify what happened.

The purpose of KPI reporting is to identify why it happened, quantify business impact, and enable leadership to take corrective action.

This playbook standardizes that process across all marketplace performance reviews.
